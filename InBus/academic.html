B.Sc Aviation

B.Sc Aviation

The B.Sc Aviation is a 3-year undergraduate program designed to prepare students for technical and operational roles in the aviation industry. The program combines academic knowledge with practical training to develop a strong foundation in aviation operations, safety, and management.

With the continuous growth of the global aviation sector, this course equips students with the technical skills, industry exposure, and confidence required to build successful careers in airline, airports, and aviation services.

Accreditation

National Institute of Management & Science (NIMS), in collaboration with Alagappa University (A State University accredited with A+ Grade & Category–1 by UGC), offers the B.Sc Aviation program.

Eligibility
