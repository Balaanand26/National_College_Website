Admission Gateway Is Now Opened For 2026 - 2027

National College invites applications for UG and PG programs for the academic year 2026–27 for our Madurai and Tirunelveli Campuses. Our UG & PG Programs include BBA-Airline & Airport Management, MBA-Airline & Airport Management, B.Sc-Aviation, BBA-Shipping & Logistics, B.Sc-Fashion Designing, B.com-Banking, Financial Services & Insurance, MBA-HR | Marketing | Finance | Operations | Business Analytics Shipping & Logistics & more.

Students can benefit from an industry-oriented curriculum, placement support, and skill-based training. Limited seats only available.

Offline Application forms can be received directly/in person from our college campus.

Candidates who have passed the qualifying 12th/UG examination will be selected on the basis of marks obtained in our Future Leaders Scholarship Entrance Exam, a direct interview, and adherence to all eligibility criteria and the availability of seats.

Date of counselling or direct interview/online platform will be intimated to the candidate through email.

Government of India in consultation with RBI and Indian Bank's Association has framed an educational loan scheme to ensure that no deserving student in the country is deprived of higher education for want of finances. The scheme covers all types of courses including professional courses in India and abroad. Eligible students can apply in respective banks. Our Student’s Relationship Manager will guide our students to obtain 100% education loan with leading banks.

Accommodation is available for boys and girls in separate hostels. The students may make a choice of a particular hostel in consultation with college authorities. However, the hostel chosen must be on the approved list of the college authorities. Students are requested to pay the hostel fee directly at the hostel. Inmates must follow the rules and regulations of the hostel.

The students who have been selected in our Future Leaders Scholarship Entrance Exam/direct interview must report to the college on the stipulated date and time as informed by the office, along with the following

Copies of mark lists and proof of identity, age, qualification etc.

Four copies of recent passport size photographs.

The students on reaching the college are required to meet our Student’s Relationship Manager with the duly filled-in application form for enrolment. Our Student’s Relationship Manager then directs the student to the interview board. The students who are selected by the board will be directed to the accounts department for payment of fees. The parent and student must read and understand the terms and conditions form and course details before joining and the same must be signed and submitted to the administrator along with admission application form.

Genuine placement is provided with a view to assist the students with high academic attainments for getting placements in International Airline & Airports, International Banks, Shipping & Logistics companies and top international Companies.

Wearing a uniform is compulsory on all working days since it brings every student onto the same platform. This inculcates a feeling of equality among the students. The industry also demands it.

Our National College students are expected to behave well and maintain discipline on campus. Any complaint about discipline will be viewed seriously and appropriate action will be taken.

Our college training faculties are powered by a team of Ph.D.-qualified faculty members only, complemented by industry expert trainers who bring real-world knowledge into the classroom.
