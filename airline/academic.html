BBA Airlines & Airport Management

BBA Airlines & Airport Management

Our BBA in Airlines & Airport Management Course is a 3-year Full Time undergraduate program designed to prepare students for exciting careers in the aviation industry. The curriculum is aligned with industry requirements and focuses on practical knowledge, operational skills, and professional development.

With the rapid growth of the aviation sector in India and globally, this program equips students with the skills and confidence required to work in airports, airlines, and aviation-related services.

Accreditation

National Institute of Management & Science (NIMS), in collaboration with Alagappa University (A State University accredited with A+ Grade & Category–1 by UGC), offers the BBA program in Airlines & Airport Management.
